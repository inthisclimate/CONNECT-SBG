# Spectral and Taxonomic Diversity Analysis

Analysis of relationships between field-measured spectral diversity
and taxonomic diversity in intertidal quadrats.

## Sampling design

- 10 transects per site
- Quadrats sampled every 10 m from the shoreline
- Three spectral measurements per 1-m² quadrat
- Spectralon white-reference measurements used to convert radiance
  to relative reflectance
- Taxonomic richness and percent cover estimated from quadrat photographs

## Analysis goals

1. Calculate multiple measures of within-quadrat spectral diversity.
2. Test relationships between spectral diversity and taxonomic richness.
3. Evaluate the influence of algal cover and distance from shore.
4. Compare univariate and multivariate spectral diversity metrics.
